Contact Our Support Team ➝

Can Qansa's IoT solutions integrate with our existing legacy systems?

Absolutely. We specialize in retrofitting and system integration. Our architecture is hardware-agnostic, meaning we can connect your existing machines (PLC, SCADA, or older ERP systems) to the cloud using custom gateways and APIs, without needing to replace your current infrastructure.

How do you ensure data security and privacy?

Security is our top priority. We implement End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) for data transmission and adhere to industry-standard protocols (such as MQTTs and HTTPS). We also conduct rigorous penetration testing on both hardware and software layers to prevent unauthorized access.
